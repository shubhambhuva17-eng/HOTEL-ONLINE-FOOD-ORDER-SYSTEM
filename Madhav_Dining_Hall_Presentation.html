Industry Challenges

Problem Statement: Traditional Restaurant Bottlenecks

Long Wait Times During Rush Hours

Customers wait 10-15 minutes just to flag down a waiter for paper menus and order placement.

×

Verbal Miscommunication & Errors

Specific dietary notes (e.g. "no onion-garlic", "mild spice") get lost or miswritten by staff.

×

Static Paper Menus & Sold-Out Friction

Paper menus cannot reflect real-time 86'd (sold-out) items, leading to customer disappointment.

×

Blind Back-of-House Operations

Restaurant managers lack real-time visibility into active table occupancy, average ticket time, and kitchen load.

×

Lack of Actionable Sales Intelligence

Paper billing receipts do not provide automated hourly, monthly, or annual item profitability insights.

×

High Labor Overhead & Turnover

Reliance on a large floor-staff team to deliver repetitive paper menus increases operational cost.

Database Schema & Fault-Tolerant Persistence

Relational Entity Schema

  • • users: Manager & kitchen chef authentication credentials.
  • • tables: Physical table mapping, seating capacities, and QR paths.
  • • categories: Starters, Main Course, Breads, Desserts, Beverages.
  • • menu: Dish details, pricing, pure-veg flag, prep time, image URLs.
  • • orders & order_items: Bill totals, status, notes, timestamps.

Guaranteed Persistence Architecture

A critical problem in student projects is data disappearing when the server reboots. Madhav Dining Hall implements an automated disk-write synchronization layer:

1. In-memory mutations trigger synchronous write to `backend/data/db.json`.
2. On server start, historical orders are fully re-hydrated.
3. Analytics remain 100% intact across server restarts and crashes.

Engineering Reflections

Key Engineering Challenges Overcome

1. Preventing Data Loss on Server Reboot

Challenge: Standard in-memory data resets on every server restart.
Solution: Implemented dual-engine persistence with automatic atomic file updates (`db.json`) keeping all daily, monthly, and yearly historical analytics intact.

2. Culinary Photographic Precision

Challenge: Generic food stock photos caused customer confusion.
Solution: Downloaded authentic local photographs matching all 10 Gujarati and Indian dishes with local zero-latency asset caching.

3. Pure Vegetarian Identity Realignment

Challenge: Retaining non-veg items conflicted with traditional dining hall expectations.
Solution: Fully purged non-veg items across seeds, APIs, and UI filters to deliver a dedicated 100% Shuddh Shakahari experience.

4. 60fps Mobile Performance Without Bloat

Challenge: Heavy external animation libraries degrade mobile browser responsiveness.
Solution: Engineered pure CSS3 keyframe animations with hardware transforms for ultra-smooth 60fps mobile execution.
